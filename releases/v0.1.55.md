- Agents can ask for a key or password in a private card in the task instead of in the chat. Wren keeps it encrypted for 24 hours, gives it to that task's commands, and blanks it out of what the task shows.
- Agents can set a task for later, like a reminder at 5 pm, and you approve each one. Schedules can now also run once at a time you pick.
- Carry a task on with another agent from its menu, or when it stops at a usage limit: the other agent starts with the conversation so far.
- Usage → This Mac shows Codex's plan limits too, and Grok Build's while its background service runs.
- Quote part of an agent's reply, put a draft aside with ⌘S (Ctrl+S on Windows), undo archiving a task, and search for exact phrases in quotes.
