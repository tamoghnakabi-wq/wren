- Fix: an update could sit on "Downloading…" forever (seen on Windows). If a download fails, Settings now says why, when Wren will try again, and offers Try again or a link to download it yourself. Wren tries again by itself after 5 minutes, then 15, then every hour.
- Updates download the way your browser does, so they work behind a system proxy and with antivirus that checks secure connections.
- An interrupted update download (dropped connection, no data for 2 minutes, or Wren quit) picks up where it stopped instead of starting over. Settings shows how far along it is.
