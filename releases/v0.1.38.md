- After you sign out of Wren in a browser, that browser no longer gets notifications about your tasks, and the ones it already shows are closed. Wren now sends notifications only to browsers that are still signed in, so this also covers a sign-in that ended some other way (signed out everywhere, removed, or expired). A browser that already had notifications on keeps getting them once you open Wren there again.
- Pressing Stop on a task that runs on your computer now takes effect at once; it could take up to 45 seconds to reach a long command or model call. A task you stop always ends as stopped.
- If Wren can't read this computer's saved settings (the file was damaged or made unreadable), agents get no permissions until you turn them on again in Settings, and Wren tells you so. Before, it fell back to the first-install settings without asking.
- In the cloud: a task that has moved to another worker no longer sends a late notification; Wren's own commands on an agent's cloud computer have time limits, end when you press Stop, and what they print is read only up to a limit; and an approved action no longer starts after a Stop that came at the very last moment.
