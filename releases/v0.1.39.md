- On a Mac, the Wren menu now has **Check for Updates…** and **Settings…** (⌘,), where other Mac apps keep them. On Windows they are under Help and File.
- Checking for updates from the menu bar or the tray now always gives an answer: you're up to date, it couldn't check (and when Wren tries again), or a newer version is downloading. If you asked, Wren tells you when that download is ready and offers to restart, or why it failed.
- When an update is downloaded, the menu item becomes **Restart to Update**. If the update can't be installed (for example, a task on this computer wouldn't stop in time), Wren now says why.
