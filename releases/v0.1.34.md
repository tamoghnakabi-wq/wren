- Agents now always ask before GitHub changes that affect who can see or reach your code: making a repository public or changing its settings, collaborators, keys, secrets, branch rules, Pages or Actions workflows, organisation and account settings, and GraphQL changes. This applies even to agents set to autonomous.
- Stopping a task now also stops a connected (MCP) tool call that hasn't been sent yet, on your computer and in the cloud.
