- Usage has a new **This Mac** tab (**This PC** on Windows). It shows how much you have used the AI coding apps on this computer (Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, Grok Build and OpenCode) without signing in to any of them.
- Wren reads only the token counts from each app's own logs, never what you wrote. The totals stay on your computer and are never sent to Wren's server.
- Pick the last 7, 30 or 90 days and switch between tokens and estimated cost. Costs are estimates at each model's API price, so a subscription can cost far less.
