- Search every task with ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows): task titles, agents, pages, and what was said in a task. Choosing a message opens the task at it.
- When your Claude or Grok plan reaches its usage limit, the task now pauses instead of failing. With Claude Code, Wren knows when the limit resets and carries the task on by itself a minute after it (you can turn this off in Settings → Usage limits, or choose "Don’t resume" on the task). Grok Build doesn’t say when its limit resets, so those tasks wait for you to resume them.
- A task the limit cut off part-way picks up where it stopped, instead of starting its request again.
- Agents can show you a page: a chart, a table or a small interactive view, right in the task. With Claude Code this is Wren’s show_page tool. Pages run in a sandbox that can’t reach your account, your files or the network.
